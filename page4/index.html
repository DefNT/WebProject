About the archive

Between history
and the imagined.

Mythos is an independent digital field guide devoted to the beings humanity created to explain the unexplainable.

Our entries bring together folklore studies, archaeology, art history, and living oral traditions. We treat every creature not as an oddity, but as a window into the people who kept its story alive.

How we work

01

Trace the source

Every entry begins with primary texts, material evidence, and recorded oral history.

02

Honor the context

We preserve local names and meanings rather than flattening stories into a single canon.

03

Show the evolution

Legends are alive. We document how each being changes as its story travels.
