About the archive

Between history
and the imagined.

Mythos is an independent digital field guide devoted to the beings humanity created to explain the unexplainable.

Our entries bring together folklore studies, archaeology, art history, and living oral traditions. We treat every creature not as an oddity, but as a window into the people who kept its story alive.

Our purpose

Why Mythos?

Myths are the oldest way people have asked big questions: where we come from, what we fear, and what we hope to protect.

Most guides reduce these beings to a picture and a one-line description. We want to keep the full story: who told it, why it mattered, and how it changed along the way. Mythos exists so that these stories stay readable, sourced, and alive.
