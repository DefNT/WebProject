Traditions

Mythologies

Six traditions, six ways of imagining the monstrous

01

Greek & Roman

Mediterranean, from about 800 BCE

Monsters exist to test heroes and gods. Known for: Medusa, Minotaur

02

Egyptian

Nile Valley, from about 3000 BCE

Composite beings guard temples and the passage of the dead. Known for: Sphinx

03

Norse

Scandinavia, about 800 to 1100 CE

Giants, wolves and serpents tied to the end of the world. Known for: Fenrir

04

Slavic

Eastern Europe, medieval folklore

Nature spirits that live in forests, rivers and homes. Known for: Leshy

05

East Asian

China and Japan

Spirits and dragons connected to water, luck and transformation. Known for: Kitsune, The Long

06

Turkic & Kazakh

Central Asian steppe

Winged horses and birds that carry heroes and good fortune. Known for: Tulpar, Samruk
